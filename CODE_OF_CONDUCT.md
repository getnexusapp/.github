# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want every space Nexus maintains — issue trackers, discussions, and
any community channels — to be welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or how
they use Nexus.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing
- Give clear, actionable feedback rather than vague criticism
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and can't act on every
  request immediately
- Respect that Nexus is closed-source — requests for source access, or
  "just open source it" comments, are off-topic for issues and will be
  redirected or closed

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Spam, off-topic self-promotion, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or any other community
  space

## Scope

This applies to all Nexus-owned spaces on GitHub (issues, discussions,
pull requests to the docs repo) and any official Nexus community
channels (Discord, forums, etc.), as well as anyone representing Nexus
in public.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to **conduct@getnexus.app** (or
getnexusupport@gmail.com). Reports are reviewed privately. Depending on
severity, consequences range from a warning to a temporary or permanent
ban from Nexus's community spaces.

Maintainers who don't enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face the same consequences.

---
This document may be adapted for other Nexus community spaces as they're
created (Discord, forums, etc.).
