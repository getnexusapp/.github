## About Nexus

Most people juggle three separate apps just to think: a notes app, a browser, and a chatbot tab somewhere else — none of them talking to each other, none of them remembering what the others know.

Nexus throws that away and starts over.

It's your notes, a real web browser, and an AI assistant, living in the same window — and actually aware of each other. Open a page, read something interesting, and just ask Nexus Cloud AI: *"Does this contradict what I already wrote?"* Nexus already knows what's in your notes and what's open in every one of your browser tabs. No copying, no pasting, no re-explaining yourself to a chatbot that's never seen your work before.

**Browse → Research → Write → Connect → Ask.**

That's it. That's the whole loop, and it happens without ever leaving the app.

## Your Notes Never Leave Your Machine

Everything you write — every note, folder, tag, link, and version — lives in a single file on your own computer. Not on a server somewhere.

Close your Wi-Fi entirely and keep working — Nexus won't even notice. Typing, organizing, tagging, linking, searching your own notes, browsing your history, exporting to PDF or Markdown, backing up your whole workspace — all of it works exactly the same with no connection at all. Even the AI-powered search that finds notes by *meaning* instead of exact words runs quietly on your own device.

## Features

### Notes That Think Like You Do

- A real WYSIWYG editor — bold looks bold, headings look like headings, code lights up as you type. No raw markdown symbols cluttering your screen.
- Paste code from anywhere and Nexus recognizes it instantly, wrapping it in a clean, highlighted code block automatically.
- Type `[[note's title]]` and it becomes a living link. Or don't even bother with the brackets — just mention another note by name in your writing, and Nexus quietly connects them for you — displayed in the Graph tab.
- `#tags` appear the moment you type them. No tag manager, no setup.
- Folders, pinning, and a lightning-fast command palette (`Cmd/Ctrl+K`) that finds any note in a heartbeat.
- A real Trash, not a delete button with no way back.

### A Browser That Belongs to Your Workspace

- Up to 8 tabs, genuinely alive — not a toy iframe that half the internet refuses to load.
- Bookmarks, history, downloads — all where you'd expect them.
- Switch away from a tab and come back later to find it exactly as you left it: scroll position, playing video, half-typed form, all intact.

### An Assistant That Actually Knows Your World

- Ask it anything, and it answers using what's actually in your notes and whatever you currently have open in the Browser tab — not a generic answer pulled from nowhere.
- It quietly watches the pages you're reading against what you've already written, and taps you on the shoulder the moment something doesn't add up.
- Keep as many separate conversations going as you like, each one saved, each one exactly where you left it.
- Turn any answer into a brand-new note with a single click.

### A Map of Everything You Know

- A living, breathing, drag-and-zoom map of how your notes connect to each other.
- Solid lines for links you made on purpose, dashed lines for connections Nexus noticed on its own.
- Watch your own thinking take shape as a shape you can actually see in the Graph tab.

### It Looks However You Want

- Four built-in themes, from near-black to properly bright, so Nexus fits your eyes and your hour of the day.

### You Own What You Make

- Exporting and backing up are things *you* choose to do — never something happening quietly in the background without your say.
- Take every note with you as plain, portable Markdown files, or turn any single note into a polished PDF.
- Back up your entire workspace to one `.zip` file, and restore it later with Nexus checking your back's health before it trusts it.
